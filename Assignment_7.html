Assignment 7 of 8 · Annotation Systems

Instructions

Step 1 · Layer Creation

Open your master CAD file from Assignment 6, open the Layer Properties Manager (LAYER), and create the following layers with distinguishable colors and By Layer plot properties:

A-ANNO-DIMS

  • Interior / exterior dimensions

A-ANNO-TEXT

  • Room names, clarification notes, general text

A-ANNO-LEDR

  • Furniture, fixture, and material callouts

Step 5 · Annotation & Dimension Application

  1. General Interior Dimensions: apply dimension strings along major interior walls, corridors, and clearance zones across all floor plans using continuous dimensioning (DIMCONTINUE). Ensure dimensions do not cross text or primary line geometry
  2. 12 Furniture & Fixture Annotations: place 12 Multileader Callouts (4 callouts for each of the three drawing scales) specifying key furniture or plumbing fixtures (e.g., "ADA Toilet with Grab Bars," "ADA Shower")
  3. Clarification Notes: place 3 General Clarification Notes (1 note per scale view) to denote code, accessibility, or construction specifications

Evaluation Rubric

Total possible score: 200 points across 3 criteria.

1. Dimensioning Precision & Technical Setup (90 pts)

Excellent (100%)
76–90 pts

All interior and overall dimensions use correct Architectural units. Dimensions are clear, aligned, properly offset, and do not cross geometry or text. Dimension styles strictly adhere to the required scale settings across both Model Space and Layout Space.

Proficient (80%)
46–75 pts

Most dimensions use correct Architectural units with minor alignment errors or slight overlapping with line geometry. Dimension styles are correctly assigned to target scales with minor scaling discrepancies.

Developing (60%)
0–45 pts

Dimensions frequently lack proper precision, overlap drawing geometry, or cross text. Scale settings in Model or Layout space show significant dimension sizing issues.

2. Multileaders & Callout Accuracy (50 pts)

Excellent (100%)
43–50 pts

All required Multileader callouts are correctly placed using designated leader styles. Landing lines, arrowheads/dots, and leader lines are neatly aligned, visually consistent, and accurately identify key furniture, fixtures, or code requirements.

Proficient (80%)
26–42 pts

Required multileaders are present but show minor inconsistencies in leader landing orientation, arrowhead sizing, or alignment. Most furniture and fixture callouts are accurately identified with minor formatting errors.

Developing (60%)
0–25 pts

Fewer than 10 multileader callouts are included, or multileaders lack defined leader styles. Arrowheads and landing lines obscure drawing features or appear mis-scaled.

3. Text Formatting, Notes & Layer Management (60 pts)

Excellent (100%)
51–60 pts

All text styles use consistent font sizes and fonts across all scales. All required clarification notes are clearly placed. All annotations reside strictly on dedicated layers, with ByLayer properties enabled.

Proficient (80%)
31–50 pts

Text styles use the specified font but exhibit slight variations in text height or margin alignment. All clarification notes are present. Annotations are mostly placed on the correct layers, with isolated instances assigned to the wrong layers or properties.

Developing (60%)
0–30 pts

Text fonts or heights are inconsistent, illegible, or unscaled for print layout. Clarification notes are missing or incomplete. Annotation elements are placed on primary geometry layers or Layer 0 without proper layer organization.
